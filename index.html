Project Overview & Architecture

Engineered by Minorens G R — 2nd Year Student, Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

1

Sensor Signal Acquisition

Employs industrial analog and digital probes (pH electrodes, NTC thermistors, galvanic DO sensors, and optical turbidity meters) connected through operational amplifiers to eliminate signal noise before sampling by the microcontroller ADC.

2

Closed-Loop Control Logic

The embedded microcontroller continuously computes threshold deviations. If pH drops below 7.0 or DO falls under 5.0 mg/L, automated relay drivers trigger the chemical dosing pumps and high-flow aeration pumps dynamically.

3

Smart UI & IoT Telemetry

Data packets are streamed via MQTT/WebSockets to this futuristic dashboard, allowing real-time parameter visualization, historic analytics, manual override controls, and automatic anomaly alerts for aquatic maintenance.
